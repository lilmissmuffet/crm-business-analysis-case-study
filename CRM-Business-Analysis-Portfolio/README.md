# NovaTech CRM Implementation — Business Analysis Case Study

**Role:** Business Analyst  
**Industry:** B2B SaaS  
**Project type:** Simulated portfolio case study  
**Tools:** Excel, Word, Draw.io/process modelling, Figma/prototyping concepts, GitHub

## Business Problem

NovaTech Solutions currently manages leads and customer information across multiple Excel spreadsheets, email, and WhatsApp. This creates duplicate records, missed follow-ups, fragmented customer history, limited sales-pipeline visibility, and manual reporting effort.

## Business Objectives

- Centralize lead and customer information
- Improve lead ownership and follow-up management
- Maintain a consistent customer interaction history
- Improve sales pipeline visibility
- Reduce manual reporting effort
- Establish controlled access to business data

## Process

### AS-IS

**Marketing → Email → Excel → Sales Follow-up → Email/WhatsApp → Excel → Manual Reporting**

### TO-BE

**Lead → CRM → Assignment → Sales Activity → Interaction History → Follow-up → Pipeline → Reporting**
